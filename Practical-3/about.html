About StudentHub

One portal for the timetable, subjects, attendance, results and campus notices of CSE students at CSPIT, CHARUSAT.

Purpose

Students need a single place to see their academic information, and administrators need a secure way to manage student records, events and user accounts. StudentHub brings these together with role-based access.

About this practical

Practical 3 adds responsive styling to the StudentHub pages: Flexbox for Home and About, CSS Grid for Dashboard and Events, and Bootstrap 5.3 for Registration and Login.

Who uses StudentHub

  • Visitor

    Views the home page, registers and logs in.

  • Student

    Views the dashboard, timetable, attendance, results and events, and manages the profile.

  • Faculty

    Planned: manages courses, assignments and attendance.

  • Admin

    Manages student records, events and user accounts, and reviews the audit log.

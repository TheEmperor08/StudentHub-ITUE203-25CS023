Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is StudentHub?

A student portal for the CSE department of CSPIT, CHARUSAT. Read more on the About page.

Where is the CSE Department located?

In the A7 Building, CSPIT, CHARUSAT Campus, Changa.

Account

How do I create an account?

Fill in the registration form with your name, college email, course and a password.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact the portal administrator through the Contact page.

Academics

Where can I see my timetable?

The weekly timetable is on the Dashboard.

Where can I check attendance and results?

On the Attendance and Results pages.

About these pages

Why do the pages have no styling?

Practical 2 covers the HTML5 structure only. Styling is added in Practical 3.

Do the forms send data?

No. The forms are static skeletons with disabled submit buttons, because Practical 2 has no server.
